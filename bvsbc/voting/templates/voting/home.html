How it works

  1. Sign up with a username, email, and password.
  2. Verify your email with a 6-digit one-time code.
  3. Cast exactly one vote — the system blocks a second attempt.
  4. Your vote is appended as a new block to a local hash chain, so the record is tamper-evident.
  5. Check the results or explore the blockchain at any time.
